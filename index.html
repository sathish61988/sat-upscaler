FAQ

Questions people usually ask

Does Sat Upscaler upload my image anywhere?

No. The app runs locally in the browser with no backend or external API.

Which image formats are supported?

You can work with JPG, PNG, and WEBP input files and export to PNG, JPG, or WEBP.

What is the difference between the upscale modes?

Balanced is general purpose, Sharp favors crisp edges, Photo keeps a natural look, and Anime boosts line clarity.

Can I compare the result in detail?

Yes. The app includes a comparison slider, zoom controls, and a fullscreen comparison modal.
