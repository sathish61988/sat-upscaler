Focused AI workspace

Sat Upscaler is designed for quick uploads, immediate enhancement controls, responsive previews, and low-friction export.

FAQ

Does Sat Upscaler upload my image anywhere?

No. The app runs locally in the browser with no backend or external API.

Which image formats are supported?

Input supports JPG, PNG, and WEBP. Export supports PNG, JPG, and WEBP.

What happens on low-memory devices?

The app falls back to the classic enhancement path when full AI inference is not suitable.
